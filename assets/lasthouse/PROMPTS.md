# The Last House — art prompts

Make each picture with your image generator. Save it in this folder (`assets/lasthouse/`) with the file name shown, then reload the game. Any picture that's missing is drawn by the game instead, so you can add them one at a time.

## Rules for every picture

- **Original designs only.** Don't name other games, films or artists in the prompt.
- **Props and characters** (`.png`):
  - One subject, whole and centred, with its feet or base touching the bottom edge.
  - A **plain pure white background**, or a transparent one if your tool can do it. The game removes a flat white or green background when it loads the picture.
  - No floor, no shadow on the ground, no text and no watermark.
- **Viewing angle for props and characters:** a high three-quarter view from the front, looking down about 35°, to match the game camera.
- **Square pictures** (floor, rug) should be at least 1024 × 1024. The wall should be at least 1536 wide.
- **Shared style line.** Add this to the end of every prompt:

  > *realistic painted game art, dark gothic opera house drowned under the sea, gilded baroque details, damp marble, candlelight, rich detail, consistent soft top light*

## The drowned (enemies)

All of them face the viewer, slightly turned to their right, ready to attack. Keep faces gaunt and eerie rather than gory.

### `usher.png` — Drowned Usher (an ordinary foe)
> A drowned theatre usher in a waterlogged plum velvet uniform with tarnished brass buttons and a pillbox cap, pale grey-blue skin, seaweed tangled on the shoulders, holding a broken brass lantern like a club. Thin and hunched. Water dripping.

### `hound.png` — Bilge Hound (a fast foe)
> A gaunt hound-like creature from a flooded orchestra pit: slate-blue hide slick with water, a ridge of coral spines down its back, long thin legs, a lantern-fish lure glowing faintly on its brow. Low and crouched, mid-snarl. Wider than tall.

### `voice.png` — Pit Voice (a foe that attacks from range)
> A floating spectral chorister: a tall translucent violet figure in a tattered choir robe that fades into mist below the waist, a cracked porcelain mask, and floating musical notes glowing lilac around its open hands.

### `reaver.png` — House Reaver (a heavy foe)
> A huge armoured stagehand turned brute: rusted iron plates riveted over a crimson coat, a stage counterweight on a chain held as a flail, a bucket-like helm with a narrow glowing slit, broad shoulders crusted with barnacles. Imposing and heavy.

### `impresario.png` — The Drowned Impresario (the final boss)
> A towering drowned opera impresario in a long black tailcoat with gold embroidery gone green, a tall cracked top hat, a cape of torn red stage curtain, and a gilded conductor's cane topped with a pearl. Pale, regal, sinister, with water and kelp streaming from his sleeves. Grand pose, about twice a person's height.

## Checking your pictures in the game

1. Open the game from the local server, not by double-clicking the file. Browsers block the background removal on pictures opened straight from disk.
2. Go to **The Last House** and start any call.
3. If a picture looks the wrong size, crop it tighter so the subject fills the frame. The game scales each one to a fixed height: column 3.4, enemies 1.9–3.3, candelabra 1.9, bench 1.15, rubble 0.75 floor units.
